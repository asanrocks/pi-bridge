Dictate into a real textarea

The page builds a Dictation and binds a button to it. The library owns the microphone, the session, the editing model, and the phase machine; the page owns credentials, the host, and every pixel. Audio goes from this tab straight to Doubao; only the device credential comes from the local server.

idle

Tap the button on a desktop and press again to commit; press and hold it on a touch screen and release to commit. Type into the field while speaking to see the dictation drop itself instead of overwriting your edit.
